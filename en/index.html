Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is the price worked out?

It depends on the type of service, the size of the room or furniture, how dirty it is, the amount of work and the equipment needed. For an exact price, send us a photo on WhatsApp.

Will every stain come out?

It depends on the type and age of the stain and on the fabric or surface. Some stains may not come out completely, and discolouration or marks from earlier cleaning attempts can remain. We tell you what result to expect before we start.

Do you take the furniture away?

Most upholstery is cleaned on site, in your home. Whether anything needs to be moved depends on its size and the type of service.

How long does furniture take to dry?

Drying time depends on the fabric, the cleaning method, ventilation and conditions in the room. We recommend not using the furniture until it is completely dry.

Can you clean in homes with children, older people or pets?

We choose products based on the material and the type of dirt, and use them only as intended and in the right amounts. We take extra care in homes with children, older people or pets. After cleaning, we recommend airing the room and letting the furniture dry fully.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

That depends on the service and what we agree in advance. What matters is that we can get in and have what we need to do the job.
